Current Focus

Most of my time goes to Clockwork, the AI operations platform I built for my company, Beat the Clock. It gives trades businesses an agent that catches the call they missed, drafts the reply in their voice, prices the quote off their own rate card, chases the follow-up, and collects the invoice — with the owner approving every message before it goes out. Building it has meant getting serious about Python, prompt engineering, and the unglamorous discipline of making an AI system fail honestly instead of guessing. This fall I start at UIUC studying Finance and Data Science, which will keep me fluent in the numbers a business actually runs on.
