Clockwork

Architect & Sole Engineer · Late 2025 – Present

The AI operations platform Beat the Clock runs on. Work leaks out of a trades business at ten different points in a day — the call you missed, the quote you meant to send, the follow-up you forgot, the appointment nobody confirmed, the invoice in your glovebox. Clockwork closes every one of them, and an AI agent named Tock drafts the work so the owner only has to approve it.

Ten automated stages, eight screens, one rule: Tock drafts, you send.

ENGINEERING DECISIONS THAT MATTER

  • Prices are computed, never generated. Quotes come from the owner's rate card, so the model can't hallucinate a number that costs someone money.
  • Failure is never recorded as an answer. An unreachable model is a distinct, retryable state — not a degraded reply filed as finished work.
  • Ten voice laws, enforced in CI. The agent's writing is governed by rules the test suite checks; break one and the build fails.
  • Every send is on the record. Nothing goes out without an outbox row carrying a timestamp and a reason.
  • Earned autonomy. The agent only graduates to sending a message type on its own after enough drafts are approved unedited — a deliberate two-step switch, revocable in one tap.
  • Multi-tenant from day one. Scales from a single owner-operator to multi-location businesses with role-separated access and strict data isolation between locations.
