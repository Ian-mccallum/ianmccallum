Work leaks out of a trades business at ten points in a day: the call that went unanswered, the quote that never left, the forgotten follow-up, the unconfirmed appointment, and the invoice still in the glovebox. Clockwork closes those gaps while keeping the owner in control.

An AI agent named Tock drafts the work in the owner’s voice. The operating rule is precise: **Tock drafts, you send.** Automation is earned by message type, not assumed globally, and it can be revoked in one tap.

The hard part is not producing an answer. It is knowing when the system should refuse to guess. Pricing stays deterministic, failures remain visible and retryable, opt-outs are enforced on every send path, and the outbox makes every customer-facing action auditable.
