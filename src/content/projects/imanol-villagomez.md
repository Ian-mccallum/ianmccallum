Ian built a custom portfolio website for concert videographer Imanol VillaGomez, whose live-show work includes artists such as Charli XCX, Playboi Carti, and The Hellp.

The site uses a bold, visual-first presentation suited to fast-moving music and nightlife footage, letting the work lead instead of burying it inside a conventional agency layout.
