# Background Setup Instructions

## ✅ Current Status

The website is now set up to use a **single, perfect AI-generated background image** that combines:
- Futuristic city (distant)
- Ocean/water (midground)
- Blue sky with clouds (top)
- Rolling green hills (foreground - NOT covering whole ground)

## 🎨 Generate Your Background Image

1. **Read the prompt guide**: Open `AI_BACKGROUND_PROMPT.md` for detailed AI generation prompts
2. **Choose your AI tool**: Midjourney (best), DALL-E 3, Leonardo.ai, or Stable Diffusion
3. **Generate the image** using the provided prompts
4. **Save the image** as: `img/frutiger-background.jpg`

## 📁 File Requirements

- **File Name**: `frutiger-background.jpg`
- **Location**: `img/frutiger-background.jpg` (in the `img/` folder)
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1920x1080, preferably 3840x2160 (4K)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (landscape)
- **Format**: JPG (optimized) or PNG

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Generate your AI background image
2. Save it as `img/frutiger-background.jpg`
3. Refresh your browser
4. The background will automatically appear!

## 🔍 Testing Without Image

If you want to test the layout before generating the image, you can temporarily use a placeholder:

1. Find any panoramic landscape image online
2. Save it as `img/frutiger-background.jpg`
3. The site will use it as the background

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- The image MUST be named exactly: `frutiger-background.jpg`
- It MUST be in the `img/` folder
- The image should be high resolution for best quality
- Make sure the composition matches your vision:
  - Hills in foreground (bottom 30%)
  - Ocean visible in midground
  - City distant on horizon
  - Blue sky with clouds at top

## 🎯 Composition Checklist

Your generated image should have:
- ✅ Rolling green hills in foreground (NOT covering entire bottom)
- ✅ Ocean/water visible between hills and city
- ✅ Futuristic city skyline in far distance
- ✅ Vibrant blue sky with white clouds
- ✅ Frutiger Aero aesthetic with saturated colors
- ✅ 16:9 aspect ratio
- ✅ High resolution (1920x1080 minimum)
