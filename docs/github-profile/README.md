Founder of [Beat the Clock](https://www.beatyourclock.com/) and the engineer behind **Clockwork**, software for home-service teams.

I'm from Naperville, Illinois, and study Finance and Data Science at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign's Gies College of Business. I work across software, business, and design: understanding a problem, building something useful, and improving it through real use.

## What I'm building

**[Clockwork](https://www.ianmccallum.com/portfolio/clockwork)** is my main focus. I design and build the product through Beat the Clock, with an initial focus on residential HVAC service and repair. The current application supports lead intake, follow-ups, and quote and invoice workflows. It combines human-reviewed drafts with configured automatic messages; the deeper residential service workflow is still in development.

I'm especially interested in AI systems with clear boundaries: visible actions, review where it matters, and failures people can understand and recover from.
